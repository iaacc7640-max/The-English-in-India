## Facts: checked so far, and still to check
Checked on Wikipedia this session:
- Pondicherry fell to the British in 1761, after Wandiwash (1760) [Carnatic wars].
- Siraj-ud-Daulah objected to the fortification of Fort William; Calcutta was taken in June 1756 [Siraj ud-Daulah].
- Clive sailed from Madras and recaptured Calcutta; Plassey, 23 June 1757, decided by Mir Jafar's betrayal; the battle lasted about 11 hours [Battle of Plassey].
- Mir Qasim, Nawab 1760–63 [Mir Qasim]; Buxar, 22–23 October 1764, against Shah Alam II, Mir Qasim and Shuja-ud-Daula of Awadh [Battle of Buxar].
- Treaty of Allahabad, 16 August 1765: the Diwani of Bengal, Bihar and Orissa, the right to collect revenue [Treaty of Allahabad].
- The 1770 famine: 7–10 million thought to have died; the Company did not reduce taxes [Great Bengal famine of 1770].
- The 1773 Regulating Act made Warren Hastings Governor-General of Bengal [Regulating Act 1773]. Narration says "first Governor-General", which still needs "of Bengal" added.
