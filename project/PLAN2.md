## Facts (all checked on Wikipedia this session; full list goes into script.json "sources")
- Carnatic wars: Wandiwash 1760, Pondicherry fell 1761 · Siraj-ud-Daulah: objected to the Fort William fortification; Calcutta taken June 1756.
- Battle of Plassey: Clive and Watson from Madras recaptured Calcutta; 23 June 1757; about 11 hours; Mir Jafar's betrayal; a GBP 3 million settlement (EIC page).
- Mir Qasim, Nawab 1760–63 · Battle of Buxar, 22–23 October 1764 (Shah Alam II, Mir Qasim, Shuja-ud-Daula of Awadh) · Treaty of Allahabad, 16 August 1765: the Diwani of Bengal, Bihar and Orissa.
- The 1770 famine: 7–10 million thought to have died; the Company did not reduce taxes · Regulating Act 1773: Hastings made Governor-General of Bengal.
- Anglo-Mysore wars: four wars; Tipu killed at Seringapatam, 1799 · Subsidiary alliance under Wellesley: a Company army kept in the state, paid for by the ruler.
- Third Anglo-Maratha War 1817–19 (Company control of Maratha lands by 1818) · Punjab annexed 1849 · Doctrine of Lapse under Dalhousie: Satara 1848, Jhansi 1853, Nagpur 1854 · First passenger train Bombay–Thane, 1853.
- 1857: rumours of cow and pig fat on cartridges; Meerut, 10 May 1857; Bahadur Shah Zafar declared emperor; exiled to Rangoon (Dec 1857), end of the Mughal dynasty; Lakshmibai lost her kingdom through the Doctrine of Lapse; atrocities on both sides; contained June 1858; amnesty 1 Nov 1858.
- Government of India Act, 2 August 1858 · EIC dissolved 1874 · Victoria proclaimed Empress, 1 January 1877 · Naoroji's drain theory.
- Congress founded 28 December 1885, first session in Bombay; petitions and resolutions · Bengal partition announced by Curzon 16 October 1905; Swadeshi and boycott; reversed 12 December 1911; capital moved to Delhi.
- Gandhi returned 1915 · Jallianwala Bagh, 13 April 1919, unarmed crowd, 379 to 1,500+ killed · Non-cooperation launched 4 September 1920: foreign cloth abandoned, titles returned, posts resigned.
- Salt March, 12 March – 6 April 1930, 387 km (about 240 miles), Sabarmati to Dandi; worldwide news coverage.
- Quit India, 8 August 1942; almost all Congress leaders imprisoned; INA under Bose from South-East Asia · Indian Independence Act, Royal Assent 18 July 1947 · India and Pakistan at midnight 14–15 August 1947 · 12–20 million displaced.
- Not checked: Britannica (it blocks automated readers) and Ahir's book (I don't have the PDF here).
