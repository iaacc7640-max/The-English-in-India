## Fact check: what changed from the draft (needs your OK)
1. **Hooghly 1686, changed.** The draft said "Mughal forces sacked the English base at Hooghly". The sources don't support that.
   Wikipedia (Anglo-Mughal war) says fighting broke out at Hooghly in 1686: a brawl in the market, then an English force sent against the town
   (marked "citation needed"). The Mughals massed troops, and Charnock withdrew to Sutanuti (20 Dec 1686) and then Hijli. Ahir's chapter (as I recall it;
   I could not re-open the PDF here) says the *English* sacked Hugli in 1686. New line: **"In sixteen eighty-six, fighting broke out at Hooghly,
   and the English were driven out of the town."** Visuals unchanged: far flames, the Company flag falls.
2. **"Twelve hundred rupees" (1698), weakly sourced.** It is in your brief and attributed to Ahir. Online I found it only on one timeline site.
   The 9 Nov 1698 deed and the seller (the Sabarna Roy Choudhury family) are confirmed, but the price is not. Options: keep it and credit Ahir, or drop the number.
3. **Golden Farman terms.** Sources differ ("500 pagodas a year" vs "a fixed customs duty"). Kept as in the brief, credited to Ahir.
4. Everything else checked out: see Sources at the end.
